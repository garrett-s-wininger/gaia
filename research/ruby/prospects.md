# Ruby research prospects

The full SHA-256 provider improvement is measured. Do not remove verification or quietly preload OpenSSL outside the request when comparing another implementation.

A useful next question is whether unused payload/index state can stay out of Ruby objects and out of the initial read. Compare bounded lazy/native metadata with warmed Bootsnap, retaining complete artifact admission, ordinary Ruby require behavior and all compatibility costs. Include source inspection, native extensions, mutable/load-path handling, ABI rebuilds, producer cost and memory. The current pack's immutable-input contract is narrower than arbitrary editable dependencies.

Another tool or larger module graph is useful only with a pinned real operation and a competent cache baseline. Initialized-session reuse would be separate lifetime/isolation research; it must not be called cold archive startup.

The [first report](../../experiments/ruby_startup/results/initial/REPORT.md) preserves the original negative result, diagnostic and bounded provider refinement. No additional optimization is claimed.
